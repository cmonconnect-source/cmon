FAQs

What is FMC-21?

A 21-day English fluency challenge presented by C'mon.

Who can join?

Anyone who wants to improve English communication, confidence and fluency.

Do I need perfect English?

No. FMC-21 is designed to help you improve.

What happens if I miss a day?

FMC-21 follows a strict no-miss rule. Missing a day can disqualify you from certification, and the fee is non-refundable.

Can I join Level 2 or Level 3 directly?

No. Level 2 is for Level 1 certificate holders, and Level 3 is for Level 2 certificate holders.
